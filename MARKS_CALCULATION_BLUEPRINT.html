3. Formative Assessment (FA1, FA2, FA3, FA4) Calculation Formula

Har Formative Assessment 4 specific CCE components me divide hota hai:

1. Reading & Reflection Max 5 Marks (25%)
2. Written Work Max 5 Marks (25%)
3. Project Work Max 5 Marks (25%)
4. Slip Test Max 5 Marks (25%)

* Mathematical Formula: $\text{Slip Test} = \text{Round}(M \times 0.25)$, $\text{Project} = \text{Round}(M \times 0.25)$, $\text{Written} = \text{Round}(M \times 0.25)$, $\text{Read/Refl} = M - (\text{Slip} + \text{Proj} + \text{Writ})$. Total always equals entered mark $M$.

📗

4. Summative Assessment (SA1 & SA2) Calculation Formula

TOTAL SA1+FA (100M) = SA1 Written (80M) + FA Average (20M)
SA1 FA Average Formula:

$\text{FA Avg} = \frac{\text{FA1} + \text{FA2}}{2}$ (ya sirf FA1 agar FA2 abhi conduct nahi hua hai).

SA2 (Annual) FA Average Formula:

$\text{FA Avg} = \frac{\text{FA1} + \text{FA2} + \text{FA3} + \text{FA4}}{4}$ (conducted FAs ka average).
